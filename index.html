About Me:

doing tasks for work and for fun:
I like numerical simulations, low-level programming, and interpretable machine learning modelling. I find great joy in a well automated and user friendly spreadsheet and a good graph! I love to solve problems. I problems as inherently solvable (within reason) and will make a spirited and relatively well documented series of attempts to solve them until I reach an acceptable end result. I am most motivated by environmental, biological, and engineering problems with real-world concrete and practical impacts. My primary goal throughout any work I do is to help people and the environment, and I believe these things must often go hand in hand.

playing and having hobbies:
I also like frolicking in nature and taking pictures of flowers and mushrooms. One day, I want to restart my hobby of wheel throwing. I've was very involved in the ACM, Society of Women in Computing, Linux, and Robotics clubs during undergrad. I also enjoy robotics and was very dedicated to the CAD, mechanical, and outreach subteams of my FIRST Robotics Competition team from 6-12th grade and I now go back when I can to mentor.

some of my favorite things:
My favorite animal is the gar and my favorite type of fungus is arbuscal mycorrhizal fungi, such as Rhizophagus irregularis. Investigations to find my favorite color remain ongoing.
